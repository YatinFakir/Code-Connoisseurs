Automated Workforce Management Solution

Code Connoisseurs delivers an automated workforce management platform for Azania Night Lounge, featuring intelligent shift scheduling, secure QR-based attendance tracking, real-time leave management, and automated payroll integration. Our system eliminates manual errors, prevents scheduling conflicts, and provides data-driven insights to optimize staff allocation and enhance operational efficiency in dynamic environments.

The Project

Mobile App - Employee View

Automated Shift Scheduling & Attendance Tracker

Developed for Azania Night Lounge, our system automates workforce management by eliminating paper-based processes, manual scheduling conflicts, and attendance tracking errors. The solution provides real-time insights and automated shift allocation based on employee availability, roles, and business needs.
